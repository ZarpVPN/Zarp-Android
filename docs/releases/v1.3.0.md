## What's new

- **Faster one-button connections.** Zarp tries the saved strategy and verified alternatives first, then connects with the first new strategy that passes two independent checks. If that strategy fails when applied, the search continues with the next candidate.
- **Quick Scan still finds three strategies.** The separate **Quick Scan** button keeps its default limit of three confirmed working strategies and chooses the best result. **Full Scan** still tests the entire set.
- **No endless background searches.** Connection recovery retries the current strategy at most three times, with a timeout for each attempt. It no longer starts a new strategy scan in the background or discards verified results when recovery fails.
- **Reliable cancellation and app closing.** Closing the app or removing it from Recents cancels unfinished searches and queued retries. An established VPN keeps running. Late checks and callbacks from an old tunnel no longer interfere with a newer connection.
- **More reliable Xray/XHTTP recovery.** Two consecutive failed traffic checks trigger recovery of the same strategy/server pair. Failed attempts clean up the old tunnel and VPN instead of leaving an incomplete connection running.
- **Fixed a foreground-service crash.** Short operations, fast cancellation and failed connection attempts now wait for Android to acknowledge the service start before stopping it. This fixes a crash reproduced on a real Android 10 device.
- **Safer saved results.** A scan chooses only strategies confirmed during that scan. Cancelling before saved results have loaded no longer risks clearing them, and immediate cancellation no longer leaves the engine locked.

Verified through USB ADB on Huawei ANG-LX1 running Android 10, including real WARP traffic, Xray/XHTTP interruption and recovery, cancellation and app closing. All 121 JVM tests passed.

Updates v1.2.0 in place using the same signing key. Existing WARP registration, settings, routing rules, per-app selections and saved connections are preserved.
